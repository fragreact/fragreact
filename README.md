# FragReact

**FragReact** is a free website that turns your CS2 matches on FACEIT into highlights. Paste a match link or your
nickname at **[fragreact.com](https://fragreact.com/en)**: it finds the kills and deaths of all ten players and plays
each moment as a first-person 3D replay in your browser, with every player's voice, opponents included.

No CS2 install, no downloads, no demo files to deal with.

## What it does

- **Finds the moments by itself.** The kills and deaths of all ten players in the match.
- **First-person 3D replay in the browser.** The killer's eyes, the victim's, or both side by side.
- **The enemies' voice chat.** Hear what the other team said right after your kill, which you can't hear during the
  match.
- **Video export.** An MP4 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts or for YouTube.

## How it works

1. Open [fragreact.com](https://fragreact.com/en) and paste a FACEIT match link, or search for a nickname.
2. FragReact analyzes the match.
3. Watch the moments, pick the best ones and export a video.
